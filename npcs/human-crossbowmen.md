# Human Crossbowmen - "Howlers" Mercenary Squad

Elite mercenary troop of a hundred human crossbowmen, the "Hundred Howlers."

Mercenary rates per squad of 10 crossbowmen:
- Guard duty: 15gp/day; 400gp/month
- Safe/highway travel: 10gp/day
- Dangerous/off-road travel: 20gp/day
- Siege support: 30gp/day
- Combat: 50gp per battle (of no more than 4 hours)

The Hundred Howlers wear a distinctive uniform of spike-covered studded leather armor and a matching helmet, making them easily recognizable on the battlefield.

The howlers prefer to hold a defensive position, firing their crossbows from behind propped-up pavises for cover. They can rapidly reload their heavy crossbows to fire one each round, benefiting from the partial cover of their pavises. If infantry or cavalry charge, they take up their longspears to defend the position. Their armor spikes are always there so they can defend themselves if enemies manage to close in, regardless of whether they had warning beforehand.

They have a signature howl they use in combat to intimidate their enemies and coordinate their initial volleys of crossbow fire.

**Prop up Pavise:** a pavise is a tower shield that can be planted for cover using an attached support leg. Planting the pavise requires a standard action. Once propped up, the shield provides stationary cover.
