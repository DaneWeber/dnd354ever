# Dragonvale (DV) Spells

## Wrinkle Reality

*   Transmutation
*   **Level:** Sor/Wiz 9
*   **Components:** V, S
*   **Casting Time:** 1 full-round action
*   **Range:** 30 ft.
*   **Area:** Emanation
*   **Duration:** Concentration
*   **Saving Throw:** Fortitude and Reflex and Will; see text
*   **Spell Resistance:** No

You cause reality to wrinkle and fold in upon itself in a poly-dimensional twist. Geometry and physics within 30 feet seem to follow different laws. Anyone within the emanation, including you, must save each round, regardless of previous success or failure, or be sickened (Fortitude save), slowed (Reflex save), and frightened (Will save).  These saves are required on the creature's turn if the creature was within the emanation at all since its last turn or enters the emanation at all on the current turn.  Furthermore, the emanation counts as a distracting spell, thus requiring Concentration checks.  You receive a +4 bonus to the aforementioned saves and checks.

Further effects within the emanation occur randomly based on a 1d12 roll each round.

| d12 | Effect                                                                  |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | A passageway/hole permanently opens where none existed before.          |
| 2   | A passageway/hole permanently closes.                                   |
| 3   | The largest rigid object suffers 10d12 damage, bypassing hardness.      |
| 4   | A random object is permanently reduced to half its size.                |
| 5   | A random object is permanently enlarged to twice its size.              |
| 6   | Gravity is reversed for one round, as per spell.                        |
| 7   | A random creature is aged 10 years, no save.                            |
| 8   | Every creature shifts 5 ft. in a random direction, determined by d8.    |
| 9   | One random creature becomes insane, Will save DC 25.                    |
| 10  | One random creature is flipped inside-out, dying, Fortitude save DC 25. |
| 11  | Everything is gated to the Far Realms, Reflex save DC 25.               |
| 12  | Roll twice more; this is cumulative.                                    |
