# Heroic Effort Rules

## Heroic Effort

When a character makes a roll against a DC or AC (not an opposed roll), they may decide that the task is so important that they are willing to push themselves beyond their normal limits to succeed. This is called a Heroic Effort.

After determining the relevant ability score, the player declares how much they are willing to risk, up to the full ability score.

If the roll is sufficient, they succeed as normal. If the roll is insufficient, but by less than or equal to the amount risked, they still succeed, but take 1 point of [ability burn](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/psionic/psionicPowersOverview.htm#abilityBurn) to the relevant ability score for each point required to succeed. If they fail by a greater margin or roll a natural 1, they take full risked ability burn even though they fail. This damage cannot be prevented or avoided in any way, although it is recovered through natural healing.

For example, if attempting a DC 15 Reflex Save with a +3 Reflex bonus, if the player risks 8 points of Dexterity and rolls a 7, they succeed, taking 5 points of Dexterity ability burn (3 + 7 + 5 = 15). If the player had only risked 4 points of Dexterity, they would have failed and taken 4 points of Dexterity ability burn (3 + 7 + 4 < 15).

No matter the roll or amount risked, this mechanism does not convert a roll into an automatic success. Concealment and other miss chances are separate and resolved after the commitment to a heroic effort, so players should exercise caution when such miss chances are in play.

Examples:

- Strength: melee attack roll, climb check, jump check
- Dexterity: ranged attack roll, Reflex save, escape artist check
- Constitution: Fortitude save, concentration check
- Intelligence: spellcraft check, disable device check, search check
- Wisdom: Will save, survival check, heal check
- Charisma: use magic device check, perform check, diplomacy check, intimidate check
