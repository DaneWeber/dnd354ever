# Kapoacinth (aquatic gargoyle)

## Ecology

In the Dragonvale setting, new gargoyles and kapoacinth begin as statues carved by non-gargoyles. Three gargoyles freeze for a year and a day while touching the statue. At the end of this period, the statue comes to life as a new gargoyle. Note that gargoyles have organs and are alive, even though they do not need to eat, sleep, or breathe.

Not listed in their usual entry: gargoyles and kapoacinth do not age while frozen in place. While they do have a finite lifespan, it can be dramatically extended by remaining frozen in place, pondering and watching the world go by. Gargoyles and kapoacinth choose to freeze in place whenever possible and naturally exercise economy of action.
