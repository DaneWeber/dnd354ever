# Coyote (Chaos Hound)

COYOTE/CHAOS HOUND CR X {DJW}
The grinning mammalian creature before you is colored most unnaturally, albeit in an oddly attractive pattern. It might be a cat or a dog or a bear of some sort, but it is certainly none of them. Bits of it disappear and reappear, and it walks through objects.

**Evaporate (Su):** The coyote can choose to be entirely incorporeal, entirely corporeal, or partially each as a free action on its turn (may do so multiple times, allowing it walk through a wall, for example, being corporeal on one side, incorporeal through the wall, and corporeal again on the other side). It may also change corporeality as a reaction once per round, allowing it to avoid an attack by becoming incorporeal or becoming corporeal in order to make an attack of opportunity.

When incorporeal or partially incorporeal, the coyote can appear in total (although True Seeing reveals what is not corporeal), or the incorporeal parts of the coyote can be invisible.

When partially incorporeal, the coyote chooses which portions of itself are corporeal and which are incorporeal. Effects that would only affect corporeal or would only affect incorporeal creatures treat the coyote as one size category smaller. Effects that affect both corporeal and incorporeal apply benefits/penalties from both types at half-rate (thus only half of the coyote's natural armor applies, and only half of its incorporeal deflection bonus).

When corporeal, the coyote can engage in physical actions, including tripping and grappling. When partially corporeal, it can still engage in physical actions, but as if it were a creature one size category smaller.

When incorporeally hiding within a solid object, the coyote can partially emerge and become be corporeal outside the object, taking cover within the solid object. In this partial form, corporeal-only effects treat the coyote as entirely corporeal, but with cover.

The coyote can be corporeal on either side of a solid object, thus increasing its size or making itself relatively immovable with regard to bull rushes, trips, grapples, and the like.

**Graspable (Ex):** If the coyote attempts to become incorporeal while grappling, any opponents presently grappling may choose to attempt a grapple check in order to maintain a grip. If they succeed, they become incorporeal with the coyote. Should they ever lose their grip on the coyote, they return to corporeal form wherever they presently are.

**Eventual Immunity to Transformation (Su):** Mortal magic cannot permanently affect or fix a coyote’s form. Effects such as polymorphing or petrification force the creature into a new shape, but beginning at the start of its next turn, it has a 20% chance to immediately return to its true form as a swift action.

**Hostile Teleportation (Ex):** When a coyote is already grappling an opponent, the coyote may engage in a special grappling action. As a standard action, may cast any spell or spell-like ability that allows the caster to teleport willing creatures, and may teleport the grappled creature as though it were willing unless the grappled creature succeeds at an opposed grapple check. The teleportation spell must have a normal casting time of one standard action or less. This ability does not prevent non-grappled opponents from attempting to disrupt the spell or spell-like ability.
