## Bandolier

[https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bandolier](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bandolier)

**Bandolier:** This leather belt has loops or pouches for carrying small items (up to dagger _or flask_ size). It is usually worn across the chest. It holds eight items.
0.5 gp, 0.5 lbs. (source: Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting)

**Bandolier, Masterwork:** This well-crafted bandolier holds twelve items.
50 gp, 0.5 lbs. (source: Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting)

**Bandolier, Reinforced:** This masterwork bandolier is reinforced with padding and metal fittings to provide protection against breaking or crushing the items when hit in the chest or falling prone (either immune or +4 on save).
80 gp, 1 lbs. (source: Homebrew)

## Potion Belt

[Realms Help > Goods & Services > Potion Belt](https://www.realmshelps.net/stores/goods.shtml#:~:text=Potion%20Belt%3A)
[Reddit for price and weight](https://www.reddit.com/r/Pathfinder_RPG/comments/1qnrk3/comment/cderf4i/)

**Potion Belt:** This sturdy leather belt similar to a bandolier has pockets shaped to hold potion vials and is fitted with ties or flaps to keep the potions from falling out. It holds six potions. Retrieving a potion from a potion belt is a ~~free action once per round~~ _swift action_.
1 gp, 1 lbs. (source: Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting)

**Potion Belt, Masterwork:** This extremely well-made potion belt holds ten potions.
60 gp, 1 lbs. (source: Forgotten Realms Campaign Setting)

**Potion Belt, Reinforced:** This masterwork potion belt is reinforced with padding and metal fittings to provide protection against breaking or crushing the vials when hit in the chest or falling prone (either immune or +4 on save).
100 gp, 2 lbs. (source: Homebrew)
