# Mundane Items

## Bandolier

https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bandolier

**Bandolier:** This leather belt has loops or pouches for carrying small items (up to dagger size). It is usually worn across the chest. It holds eight items.
0.5 gp, 0.5 lbs.

**Bandolier, Masterwork:** This well-crafted bandolier holds twelve items.
50 gp, 0.5 lbs.

## Potion Belt

[Realms Help > Goods & Services > Potion Belt](https://www.realmshelps.net/stores/goods.shtml#:~:text=Potion%20Belt%3A)
[Reddit for price and weight](https://www.reddit.com/r/Pathfinder_RPG/comments/1qnrk3/comment/cderf4i/)

**Potion Belt:** This sturdy leather belt similar to a bandolier has pockets shaped to hold potion vials and is fitted with ties or flaps to keep the potions from falling out. It holds six potions. Retrieving a potion from a potion belt is a ~~free action once per round~~ swift action.
1 gp, 1 lbs.

**Potion Belt, Masterwork:** This extremely well-made potion belt holds ten potions. Retrieving a potion from a potion belt is a ~~free action once per round~~ swift action.
60 gp, 1 lbs.

## Vial Bandolier


Potion Bandolier: This sturdy leather bandolier has pockets shaped to hold potion vials and is fitted with ties or flaps to keep the potions from falling out. It holds six potions. Retrieving a potion from a potion bandolier is a swift action.
6 gp, 1 lbs.

Potion Bandolier, Masterwork: This extremely well-made potion bandolier holds ten potions.
90 gp, 1 lbs.

Potion Bandolier, Reinforced: This masterwork potion bandolier is reinforced with metal fittings to provide protection against crushing the potions when hit in the chest or falling prone.
150 gp, 2 lbs.
