# Magic Items -- Wondrous Items

## Amazing Chest

The amazing chest seals airtight. It is 4 ft x 3 ft x 3 ft. A medium creature can easily fit inside.

It has a unique key that opens the lock (DC 40 to pick the lock). Turned clockwise it opens the chest for it’s multiple standard purposes. Turned counter-clockwise it activates the room-packing feature. Locking the chest with the key causes an Arcane Lock effect as if the one locking the chest were the caster.

Multiple Uses:
Bottom: a secret latch (Search DC 25) in the bottom of chest opens the bottom and effects a Passwall effect of a depth no more than two feet, allowing one to drop through. The bottom closes itself and relatches when it is no longer held open.
Inside front: the inside of the front of the chest can be similarly unlatched and “spun” to swap the contents of the chest with an equal volume that is held in an extradimensional space, functioning along the principles of a Bag of Holding.
Left side: the left side secret panel reveals a set of drawers. If you think of the thing you wish to find in the drawer, and it has been stored previously, it will be in the drawer. Up to a thousand and one small items may be stored in the drawers (no larger than 48” x 12” x 4”). If you think of “whatever may be in the drawer,” you will find, at random, one of the stored items. If you think of something you know is not in the drawers, and it is valued at 5gp or less, you have a 90% chance of finding one in the drawer. Each attempt lowers the chance of success by 10%. Each day that passes restores the chance of success by 10%, up to the maximum of 90%.
Right side: the right side panel features a small slot through which coins can be pushed, entering extradimensional storage, but only while holding your hand against the panel. You can withdraw the coins you have stored by holding your hand against the panel and mentally requesting the coins you want. The withdrawn coins appear either in your hand or in a held container through a teleportation effect. Only you can withdraw your coins.
Lid: the lid of the chest opens 180 degrees. It has a secret panel that opens to reveal a two-feet-deep extradimensional space that permanently conveys the effects of the Unguent of Timelessness to its contents, so long as they remain within. This allows food to be stored without spoiling, or a corpse to be kept from rotting.
Rear: the rear of the chest has an obvious latch. When the latch is turned, the entire chest and its non-living contents become incorporeal, invisible, and their magic auras become suppressed. Any object or creature not entirely within the chest is unaffected and perhaps in a surprisingly untenable position. Key holder can dismiss with a command word.

The chest can also magically pack all of a rooms furniture and other non-living accouterments into its extradimensional space and later magically unpack them.

Strong transmutation and conjuration; CL 14th; Craft Wondrous Item,; Price 14,000 gp; Weight 200 lbs.
